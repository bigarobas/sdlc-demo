title: The agent is not the point
---

Most teams have already adopted the agent. It sits in the editor, it writes plausible code,
and a human reads every line before anything happens. That works, and it stops scaling at
exactly the moment you would want it to: one person, one session, one change at a time.

The AI-native SDLC is a different claim. Not _the model got better_, but **the work moved into
artifacts and the guardrails became deterministic**. Intent, design and plan become files in
the repository. Verification becomes a command. The boundary the agent must not cross becomes
a credential it does not have.

What follows is the [playbook](https://claude.com/blog/the-ai-native-sdlc-playbook) applied to
one small real project — this website — with the parts that were faked clearly labelled as
faked.
